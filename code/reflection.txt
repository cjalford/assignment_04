I learned that a left merge keeps every row of the timesheet, even when an employee_id is not on the roster. For those rows, the roster columns such as hourly_rate_usd are filled with NaN, which happened with the employee who was missing from the roster in this assignment. When I used an inner merge, that employee's row was dropped without any error, which would have meant someone who worked hours never got paid. I also learned the lineage rule: copy-add-return keeps the input columns unchanged, and payroll_export builds a new frame instead of renaming the pipeline's columns. I used the wrong merge type at first and lost a lot of time, so next time I will read the task's business rules first and ask which side's rows must survive before I choose left, right, or inner.
